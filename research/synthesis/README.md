# Synthesis

Use `synthesis/` for cross-source distillation: patterns, contradictions,
throughlines, product bets, confidence levels, and branded Elbysodic point of
view drafts.

Synthesis is where raw signal becomes judgment. Be explicit about what
Elbysodic should accept, constrain, reinterpret, or reject.

## Template

```md
# <Synthesis Topic>

Status: working synthesis
Last updated:
Sources:
Confidence: high | medium | low | conflict

## Claim

One concise claim.

## Evidence

- Source-backed signal.

## Counter-Signal

- Conflicting source-backed signal or cultural disagreement.

## Elbysodic Interpretation

How we filter this through Elbysodic's product standards.

## Product Implications

- Accepted:
- Proposed:
- Deferred:
- Rejected:

## Promotion Target

Where this should graduate if accepted: `docs/product/`, `plans/`,
`docs/architecture/`, or `AGENTS.md`.
```
