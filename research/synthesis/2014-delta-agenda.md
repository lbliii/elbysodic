# 2014 Delta Research Agenda

Status: research agenda
Last updated: 2026-05-10

This note captures the founder baseline and the specific delta research
Elbysodic needs before turning ecosystem signal into doctrine.

## Founder Baseline

The remembered bar is 2014-era forum PBP with a durable forum backbone and
small escape hatches:

- Backbone: boards, threads, scenes, rosters, faces, claims, reserves,
  applications, wanted ads, plotters, guidebooks, skins, staff rooms, event
  prompts, archives, and director-run community ritual.
- Escape hatches: cboxes, Bravenet-style chat, AIM, IC text messages,
  phone-call scenes, OOC plotting, quick updates, and other lite rapid
  touchpoints that helped writers stay socially present between long-form
  posts.

The suspected modern state is fragmented: forums, Discord, Reddit, Tumblr,
private servers, OC/art platforms, TTRPG PbP tools, and bespoke community
workarounds each solve part of the job, but no obvious dedicated platform owns
PBP as a modern product center.

Treat this as a hypothesis until validated by public research, interviews,
community audits, and usability walkthroughs.

## Core Delta Questions

1. What changed in RP culture after Discord became the default social layer?
2. Which old forum-PBP rituals still create belonging, safety, and continuity?
3. Which old rituals were brittle workaround labor: manual claims, reserves,
   templates, apps, activity checks, and theme edits?
4. Which Discord-era habits are now expected: real-time support, proxy
   identities, quick pings, roles, tickets, channel geography, and bot-driven
   conveniences?
5. Which Discord-era habits are compensation for missing structure rather than
   product desires: channel sprawl, fragile archives, proxy confusion,
   notification fatigue, and source-of-truth loss?
6. How do Reddit partner-search norms change expectations around compatibility,
   boundaries, writing samples, ghosting, and low-commitment exploration?
7. How do Tumblr norms change expectations around selective interaction,
   mutuals, rules pages, aesthetics, tagging, asks, and boundary signaling?
8. How do TTRPG PbP norms change expectations around pace, GM authority,
   private knowledge, dice, sheets, and turn clarity?
9. What does a dedicated PBP platform need to prove before directors trust it
   with a live community?
10. What should Elbysodic deliberately refuse even if some communities ask for
    it?

## Rapid-Touch Research Track

Elbysodic should support lite rapid touchpoints only when they reinforce the
backbone.

Research separately:

- IC texts and phone calls as scene forms: transcript, participants, time,
  face, status, consent, and archive behavior.
- Chatbox/cbox equivalents: whether they are public atmosphere, OOC support,
  IC microplay, or ephemeral social glue.
- Discord-like rooms: whether they belong inside Elbysodic, as integration
  links, or as outside-community context.
- Character proxying: what Tupperbox-style behavior teaches about active face,
  quick switching, auditability, and wrong-face prevention.
- Notification cadence: which pings help writers return, and which recreate
  Discord fatigue.

Acceptance bar:

- The durable scene, face, membership, and community record remains canonical.
- Rapid touchpoints cannot become a privacy side channel.
- Quick exchanges have a clear relationship to continuity: canon, non-canon,
  OOC, draft, or archival.
- Writers can tell who is speaking, in which face, inside which community, and
  whether the exchange has story consequence.

## Evidence To Collect

- Interviews with directors who ran forum boards before 2014 and still run or
  participate now.
- Interviews with Discord-first roleplayers who have never used forums.
- Task walkthroughs for joining a modern Discord RP server, a Reddit 1x1 match,
  a Tumblr RP hub, a current forum/Jcink board, and a TTRPG PbP campaign.
- Public audits of active communities: onboarding path, application path,
  claims/reserves, character identity, scene archive, staff workflow, safety
  language, and mobile behavior.
- Alpha critique from operators who have written about PBP, community
  management, asynchronous pacing, or roleplay tooling.

## Product Translation Questions

When a source describes a desired feature, classify it before promoting:

- `backbone`: belongs in the durable Elbysodic source of truth.
- `escape hatch`: useful adjunct, but subordinate to scenes and continuity.
- `integration`: better linked or synchronized than rebuilt.
- `social convention`: better supported by copy, policy, defaults, or
  community norms than a hard product primitive.
- `reject`: conflicts with Elbysodic's safety, identity, privacy, authorship,
  readability, or PBP-native standards.
