## Claim

The follow-up confirms the first archetype system. The highest-confidence
market shape is:

- Most active public forum/Jcink ads cluster around small-town social web,
  supernatural city/town, high fantasy/court crisis, and canon-divergent AU,
  with wanted hooks acting as activation support inside those premises.
- Discord-only listings reinforce modern/urban fantasy, high fantasy, and
  structured faction play. Chatroom-like sandbox formats appear, but they are
  excluded from the premise-based seed slate.
- Reddit partner search reinforces relationship-first prompts, modern fantasy,
  romance/slow-burn, mafia/gang, fandom/original flexibility, and explicit
  compatibility metadata.
- TTRPG/PBP directories add a mechanically structured campaign branch that is
  adjacent to Elbysodic but not the center of its first seed-data pass.

The archetypes are ready to use for homepage/explore strategy and for a larger
seed-data rewrite. They are not ready for precise prevalence claims.

## Method

I sampled current public ad pages and search snippets across four source
families:

| Source family | Role in sample | Approximate observations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jcink/Tumblr ad hubs | Board and wanted-ad premises | 100+ ad slots, heavily duplicated |
| Disboard | Discord-only and Jcink-plus-Discord communities | 15+ current listing snippets |
| Reddit partner-search | 1x1 plot and compatibility patterns | 8 current/recent snippets |
| PBP/TTRPG directories | Tooling-heavy PbP and broad genre directories | 6 directory/source snippets |

After deduping repeated ads and excluding obvious affiliate-button-only links,
the follow-up used roughly 55-65 named site/server/community signals plus
several partner-search and directory signals. The exact count should be treated
as approximate because Tumblr ad hubs repost the same site across multiple days
and cross-post between hubs.

## Source Census Notes

### Small-Town Social Web

This remains the most obvious first seed category. The repeated offer is not
"real life" in the abstract. It is a community where a face can enter a town,
join family/coworker/friend webs, and get immediate scenes through events,
wanted ads, face claims, and low-friction apps.

Observed patterns:
- Fictional or real-adjacent towns: Pearl Cove, Stackhouse, Granite River,
  Brighton, Tampa Bay, Jubilee, Lynn, Evergreen Bay, Savannah, Ashbury.
- Repeated selling points: profile app, no word count, relaxed activity,
  biweekly sweeps, wanted ads, family plots, cross-plotting, member
  celebrations, Discord for plotting.
- Active-event language is common: spring festivals, farmers markets,
  challenges, mini-events, conventions, seasonal events.

Elbysodic read:
- This archetype is seed-data mandatory.
- It should stress-test claims, reserves, wanted hooks, member warmth,
  workplace/location directories, event prompts, and thread status.
- It needs many low-stakes public locations and a dense roster more than a
  heavy canon plot.

### Weird-Town Mystery

The weird-town pattern is stronger after follow-up. It combines the onboarding
ease of real-life/slice-of-life with enough supernatural or mystery structure
to sustain staff prompts, rumors, encounters, and continuity updates.

Observed patterns:
- Avior-style open-lore supernatural town with researchers, observatory,
  phenomena, prompts, and encounters.
- Coastal or isolated towns where fog, witch hunts, rumors, magical
  disturbances, or town memory shape play.
- Discord often carries lore drops and town rumors; the forum carries the
  story.

Elbysodic read:
- This is the best showcase for Continuity Graph earlier than a pure fantasy
  board, because canon can grow from threads, prompts, and rumors.
- Seed data should include public rumors, staff-reviewed truths, encounter
  prompts, and at least one mystery chapter already in motion.

### Urban Supernatural Pressure Cooker

This is distinct from weird-town mystery. It is less cozy and more about power:
species, clutches, factions, secrecy, exposure, politics, violence, nightlife,
law, prejudice, and rank.

Observed patterns:
- Vampire clutches, werewolves, witches, fae, hunters, ghosts, shifters,
  anti-supernatural laws, supernatural visibility, and faction hierarchies.
- Mature ratings and 18+/21+/25+ gates appear frequently.
- Request ads, species pages, face claims, and mature content boundaries are
  front-door signals.

Elbysodic read:
- This should be a seeded archetype, but it has more policy risk than small
  town or weird town.
- It needs species power boundaries, faction claims, content warnings, staff
  review, and clear public/private lore boundaries.

### High-Fantasy Crisis And Court Politics

The high-fantasy cluster is as dense as small-town and supernatural, but the
ads usually carry heavier lore. The most useful pattern is not "generic
fantasy." It is a crisis state: war, depleted magic, succession conflict,
faction surveillance, demon incursions, rifts, courts, relics, rebellion,
quarantine zones, or magical catastrophe.

Observed patterns:
- Current event/chapter language is prominent: blood-red snow, failing magic,
  succession crisis, rebellion 2.0, demon war, rifts, artifacts, high queens,
  border skirmishes.
- Ads lean on guidebooks, claims, wanted, Discord, magic rules, courts,
  factions, and house/rank openings.
- Many are original but inspired by recognizable fantasy/canon structures.

Elbysodic read:
- Seed data should not create a huge encyclopedia. It should create a playable
  chapter with five to seven factions, clear claims, and a concise primer.
- A fantasy seed should include enough canon roster to show politics, but not
  so much lore that first-face onboarding stalls.

### Canon-Divergent AU

The demand is undeniable across Jcink, Discord, and partner-search cultures.
Writers use canon to reduce setup cost and increase emotional buy-in. However,
an official Elbysodic seed cannot responsibly be a literal Harry Potter,
ASOIAF, ACOTAR, Dragon Age, Pokemon, Star Wars, Teen Wolf, TVD, or Warrior Cats
community.

Observed patterns:
- AU, canon-divergent, loosely based, inspired by, canons, reserves, canon
  lists, and early plotting are repeated signals.
- Canon rosters and wanted canons are major activation tools.
- Many boards age up characters, branch timelines, or remix settings to give
  OCs more agency.

Elbysodic read:
- The seed-data answer is "canon-adjacent original continuity": a realm with
  a known branch point, canons/open roles, houses/factions, and enough shared
  shorthand to feel fandom-adjacent without using protected IP.
- Explore taxonomy should support "canon-divergent" and "inspired-by" as
  metadata for user-created communities, but Elbysodic-owned seeds should be
  original.

### Fame, Industry, And Surveillance Drama

This cluster is not as dense as towns/fantasy/supernatural, but it is
strategically valuable because it stresses face identity, public/private
performance, rumor/news objects, and modern drama without requiring species or
magic systems.

Observed patterns:
- Celebrity-focused Los Angeles/New York boards.
- Real-life/celeb semi-private boards with requests and applications.
- Entertainment-industry horror/surveillance/AI-adjacent premises.
- Discord listings with stage/performance/chatroom language.

Elbysodic read:
- This is a strong fourth seed because it differentiates the slate.
- It needs a fictional-celebrity policy, public/private scene flags, gossip
  rules, career rosters, events, PR/news posts, and consent posture.

### Excluded Pattern: Multi-Genre Sanctuary Sandbox

Sandbox demand is real, especially for writers who want freedom, safety, and
low staff friction. It is also harder to market and harder to keep coherent
without good discovery, boundaries, and continuity.

Observed patterns:
- Semi-private sandbox with "write whatever your heart desires."
- Resource servers and broad directories that support many platforms and
  genres.
- Chatroom-like Discord servers where characters can meet, develop, and carry
  small verses without a single central realm.

Elbysodic read:
- This should not be a curated seed community because it is not premise-based.
- Its useful lessons should be absorbed into premise communities as safety
  posture, compatibility fields, flexible plotting, and privacy controls.

### Survival, Trials, And Structured Competition

This archetype is visible but operationally heavier. It often needs ranks,
tasks, dice, clans, packs, factions, trials, territory, mortality policy,
combat moderation, or fairness rules.

Observed patterns:
- Canine clan survival after famine, The Order, clan membership, trials.
- Warrior Cats/Jcink hybrids with territories, gatherings, and forum-first
  writing.
- Discord fantasy servers with levels, gold, routes, maps, adventure zones,
  fair leveling, dice, and event plots.
- Reddit/Discord signals around corrupt hero agencies, missions, and academy
  pressure.

Elbysodic read:
- Strong future Program Blueprint candidate.
- For initial seed data, include one structured-competition community only if
  the implementation can represent rank, event tasks, and adjudication without
  pretending manual staff labor has been solved.

### Historical Pressure

Historical pressure appears most often as a layer: wizarding 1950s, Dance of
the Dragons, medieval court, gothic Victorian, noble houses, industrialization,
crime, etiquette, class, and reform.

Observed patterns:
- 1880s London, working poverty, crime, industrialization, social reform.
- 1950s wizarding war, Marauders AU, house politics, succession politics.
- Court and noble-house fantasy with period aesthetics.

Elbysodic read:
- This deserves a seed, but probably as "historical pressure" layered with
  either city intrigue, court fantasy, or occult mystery.
- It needs explicit inclusive adaptation rules and content warnings because
  period settings often carry oppressive social structures.

### Soft Sci-Fi And Weird Frontier

The sci-fi signal is weaker in Jcink ad hubs than fantasy/supernatural, but it
gets support from broader PBP sources and directories.

Observed patterns:
- Aliens, Nevada, memory, soft sci-fi.
- Futuristic gamer-celebrity city where a virus turns players into Pokemon
  shifters.
- Daireem-style space settings with star systems, worlds, races, cargo haulers,
  bounty hunters, and a large shared universe.
- Star Army-style active plots and OngoingWorlds sci-fi categories.

Elbysodic read:
- "Strange frontier" is stronger for Elbysodic than hard sci-fi.
- Seed data should use isolation, memory, contact, frontier labor, and social
  drama more than technical simulation.

### Supporting Pattern: Wanted Hooks

This is the biggest activation pattern across sources, but it is not quite a
standalone genre. It is a discovery and onboarding mode that can sit on every
community.

Observed patterns:
- Wanted family patriarchs, siblings, fathers, arranged marriages, cowboy
  romances, death-centric witch families, family plots, canons, reserves,
  requests, open ranks, open houses, open businesses.
- Reddit partner search is almost entirely wanted-hook-first: romance, enemies
  to lovers, mafia/gang, supernatural family secrets, long-term compatibility,
  Discord, third person, age gates, post length, boundaries, and OOC
  communication.

Elbysodic read:
- Wanted hooks should be supporting objects inside premise communities.
- Seed data should include wanted hooks where they make the premise feel alive,
  but there should be no wanted-hook-first seed community or homepage lane.

## Confidence Update

Move the first synthesis from `medium` to `medium-high` for product use:

- High confidence: premise archetypes are the right taxonomy shape for home
  and explore.
- High confidence: rating/age, activity pressure, app type, Discord posture,
  and premise-specific connection hooks are front-door metadata.
- Medium-high confidence: the premise-based slate should cover town, weird
  town, supernatural city, fantasy/court, original canon-adjacent AU,
  fame/industry, survival/trials, historical pressure, and strange frontier.
- Medium confidence: relative density between archetypes.
- Low confidence: exact prevalence outside public English-language ad hubs.

## Product Implications

Accepted:
- Proceed with archetype-based homepage and exploration planning.
- Treat wanted hooks as first-class supporting objects inside premise
  communities, not as standalone community premises.
- Expand seed data to cover at least one original community per premise
  archetype, excluding sanctuary sandboxes and wanted-hook-first concepts.
- Use canon rosters in original Elbysodic seeds, but avoid literal
  copyrighted IP communities.

Proposed:
- Add `premise_archetype`, `play_engine`, `lore_aperture`, `activity_pressure`,
  `access_model`, `rating_band`, `discord_posture`, and `current_chapter` to
  future blueprint/explore metadata.
- For each seeded community, create public boards, staff-only notes, rosters,
  face claims, reserves, wanted hooks, active threads, archived threads, and
  at least one event/chapter object.
- Create premise-specific connection hooks for every seed instead of a generic
  wanted-board entry lane.

Deferred:
- Precise market sizing.
- Heavy TTRPG-style mechanics, dice, stat systems, or combat adjudication.
- Literal fandom/IP seed communities.
- Multi-genre sanctuary sandbox communities.
- Wanted-hook-first communities.

Rejected:
- Treating broad genre labels as the primary classification.
- Seeding only one "generic roleplay community" and expecting tags to cover
  the premise work.
