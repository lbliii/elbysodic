# Source Notes

Use `sources/` for public source summaries and competitive observations.

Good source notes are concise and traceable. They should capture enough signal
that future product work can understand why the source mattered without
re-reading the entire page.

## Template

```md
# <Source Title>

Status: raw source note
Source type: blog | forum | community | platform | rules | guide | article | other
Segment: forum PBP | Discord RP | Reddit 1x1 | Tumblr indie | TTRPG PbP | MMO RP | OC/art | hybrid | other
URL:
Accessed:
Confidence: high | medium | low | conflict

## Summary

Brief neutral summary of what the source says or demonstrates.

## Source Signal

- What the source directly claims, shows, or repeats.
- Include short quotes only when useful and copyright-safe.

## Inference

- What we think this means for roleplayers, directors, moderators, or platform
  builders.

## Product Implications

- Proposed:
- Accepted:
- Deferred:
- Rejected:

## Open Questions

- What needs validation?

## Related Elbysodic Surfaces

- Writer Desk, scene reader, composer, wanted, plotting, applications, claims,
  reserves, Studio, Appearance Studio, public discovery, etc.
```
