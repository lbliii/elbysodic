# UAT Protocol: Public Realm Preview

Status: reusable UAT protocol
Flow: public realm preview and request/invite posture
Primary users: Invited/New Face Applicant, Dedicated Platform Regular, Modern
Design Skeptic, Private Friend-Group Operator
Last updated: 2026-05-10

## Research Question

Can a prospective writer understand whether a realm is active, safe, modern,
and open to them before committing to an account, application, or face?

## Artifact Options

- public realm gateway route
- `/network` or public catalog card
- screenshot of public preview
- concept mock
- copy deck

## Starting State

- User is signed out or has no membership in the realm.
- User found the realm through a friend, public catalog, search result, Reddit,
  Discord, or old forum link.
- User does not yet know the community's application or invitation posture.

## Task

Figure out whether this realm is accepting new faces, what kind of story it is,
how active it feels, and what the next safe step is.

## Success Criteria

- User can describe premise, tone, activity posture, and recruitment state.
- User can tell whether access is invite-only, request-access, closed, or open
  to applications.
- User can find guidebook/application/wanted context without seeing private
  member or staff data.
- User trusts the product enough to continue.

## What The Researcher May Explain

- The product is a PBP-native studio layer.
- Some communities may be invite-first or private.

## What The Researcher Should Not Explain

- Where the primary CTA is.
- What `face`, `wanted`, `claims`, `reserves`, or `scene` means unless the
  participant asks.
- Whether the realm is active beyond what the artifact shows.

## Observation Prompts

- What do you think this community is about?
- Who do you think can join?
- What would you click next?
- What makes this feel active or inactive?
- What, if anything, makes you hesitate?
- What do you think is public versus member-only?

## Synthetic Panelists

Use:

- Invited/New Face Applicant
- Dedicated Platform Regular and Modern Design Skeptic
- Community Director
- Staff Moderator and Safety-Boundary Writer

Optional adversarial lenses:

- Discord Loyalist
- Private Friend-Group Operator
- Accessibility-First Writer

## Risks To Watch

- Public page looks like generic SaaS or dated forum software.
- Activity signals leak private member behavior.
- Recruitment state is unclear.
- Public wanted/activity copy implies a hook is open when it is socially stale.
- Request-access path feels like generic signup instead of a PBP intake path.
- Mobile hides trust cues or next action.

## Required Proof Candidates

- Rendered tests for signed-out, no-face member, ordinary member, staff, and
  director public states.
- Browser screenshots for mobile and desktop.
- Copy review for PBP-native vocabulary.
- Privacy proof for public counts, recent activity, wanted pressure, and roster
  summaries.
