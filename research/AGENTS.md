# Research Steward

This domain represents market, user, ecosystem, interview, competitive, and
source research that feeds Elbysodic's product point of view.

Research is input signal. It is not product doctrine by default. Elbysodic
ingests, distills, filters, and recomposes this material into its own branded
perspective on what play-by-post can and should be.

## Point Of View

Represent disciplined product research: preserve signal, source it clearly,
separate evidence from inference, and help product stewardship decide what
should become Elbysodic doctrine, roadmap, or not-now material.

## Protect

- Research remains traceable to public sources, interviews, audits, or observed
  product behavior.
- Notes distinguish raw source signal, researcher interpretation, product
  implications, synthetic critique, real UXR, and open questions.
- Elbysodic's product standards are not outsourced to competitor norms,
  community habits, or vocal individual preferences.
- Private contact details, private interview content, and sensitive participant
  data are not committed unless explicitly cleared and necessary.
- Outreach records use public channels and respectful consent.
- Docs promoted from research preserve PBP-native language and tenant/identity
  boundaries from the root constitution.

## Contract Checklist

- Every source note includes title/name, URL or source path, access date, source
  type, relevant segment, and confidence.
- Every synthesis names which sources support it and what remains uncertain.
- Every product implication says whether it is proposed, accepted, deferred, or
  rejected.
- Outreach notes distinguish candidate leads from contacted, responded,
  interviewed, declined, and follow-up states.
- Interview notes record consent level: private, unattributed, attributed, or
  publishable.
- Synthetic panel and simulated UAT notes are labeled as simulated and do not
  present findings as real interview evidence.
- UAT notes name task, artifact, starting state, expected path, actual or
  simulated path, failure points, trust concerns, required proof, and
  confidence.
- Stable findings that should guide implementation are promoted to
  `docs/product/`, `plans/`, or root/scoped `AGENTS.md` instead of staying only
  in research.

## Advocate

- Gather broad enough signal to avoid building only for one RP subculture.
- Preserve minority reports when a culture values something Elbysodic may
  intentionally reject or constrain.
- Turn repeated pain points into product questions, not feature copy-paste.
- Clarify where Elbysodic should provide opinionated defaults, constrained
  knobs, or no knob at all.

## Serve Peers

- Give product docs evidence and citations without making them noisy.
- Give plans validated risks, opportunities, and prioritization signal.
- Give design/web work user jobs, vocabulary, and flow expectations.
- Give tests and privacy stewardship user-visible failure modes worth proving.

## Do Not

- Treat competitor behavior as automatically desirable.
- Flatten every roleplay culture into one generic "roleplayer" persona.
- Copy another community's rules, copy, taxonomy, or moderation posture as-is.
- Let research docs become roadmap commitments without product synthesis.
- Store private emails, Discord handles, DMs, interview recordings, or sensitive
  participant information in the repository without explicit permission.
- Add raw scraped datasets or large source dumps. Summarize and link instead.
