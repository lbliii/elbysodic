# Outreach Templates

These templates are starting points. Personalize them and use public contact
channels appropriate to the recipient's platform.

## Alpha Critique Request

```text
Hi <name>,

I'm researching modern online roleplay workflows for Elbysodic, a
roleplay-native play-by-post studio focused on character identity, scene
continuity, wanted/backstage handoffs, applications, claims, reserves, and
director workflows.

Your writing/work on <topic/project> stood out because <specific reason>. We
are trying to learn from forum PBP, Discord RP, Tumblr/indie RP, TTRPG PbP,
MMO RP, OC/art communities, and hybrid operators without flattening those
cultures into a generic forum or chat product.

Would you be open to a 30-minute alpha walkthrough or written critique? The
most useful feedback would be: what feels culturally right, what would make
roleplayers distrust it, what rituals we should not lose, and what controls
should stay opinionated rather than fully configurable.
```

## Written Feedback Request

```text
Hi <name>,

I'm collecting research for Elbysodic, a roleplay-native PBP studio. I found
your public work on <source/topic> useful, especially <specific observation>.

If you are open to it, I would value short written feedback on three questions:

1. What does modern online roleplay most need from its tools?
2. What do existing platforms get wrong about identity, continuity, discovery,
   or community management?
3. What should a new PBP platform refuse to make configurable, even if some
   communities ask for it?

Happy to keep feedback private, unattributed, or attributed depending on your
preference.
```
