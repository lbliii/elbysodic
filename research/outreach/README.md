# Outreach

Use `outreach/` for public research leads, outreach templates, and contact
status. Outreach is for learning and critique, not selling.

Keep contact records respectful and minimal. Do not store private handles,
emails, or DMs unless they are public, necessary, and consented.

## Plans And Templates

- `pbp-alpha-recruiting.md`: phased recruiting plan for discovery and concept
  critique interviews.
- `templates.md`: reusable outreach messages.
- `leads.md`: candidate public leads seeded from research.
- `contact-log.md`: minimal status log; do not store private contact details.

## Status Labels

- `candidate`
- `queued`
- `contacted`
- `responded`
- `scheduled`
- `interviewed`
- `declined`
- `follow-up`
- `closed`
