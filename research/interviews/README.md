# Interviews

Use `interviews/` for protocols, consented notes, and synthesis from direct
conversations with roleplayers, directors, moderators, platform builders, and
community operators.

Do not commit recordings, private contact details, raw DMs, or sensitive
participant data unless the participant explicitly gave permission and the data
is necessary. Prefer summarized notes.

## Protocols

- `protocols/pbp-alpha-discovery.md`: first-wave discovery protocol for
  current PBP workflows, identity, safety, discovery, director burden, and
  modern expectations.
- `protocols/elbysodic-concept-brief.md`: one-page concept brief for concept
  critique after discovery context is understood, or for async written
  feedback.

## Consent Levels

- `private`: internal learning only; do not quote or attribute.
- `unattributed`: may summarize without identifying the participant.
- `attributed`: may name the participant and summarize.
- `publishable`: may quote or publish specific approved excerpts.
