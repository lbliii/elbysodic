Writers can now sign in with passkeys: identity settings (`/identity`) lets a signed-in writer add, rename, and remove passkeys, and the login page offers passkey sign-in with the password flow unchanged. Passkey sign-in establishes the exact same session and cookie posture as password login and shares the production auth rate-limit budget. Deploys should pin `ELBYSODIC_PASSKEY_ORIGIN` (and optionally `ELBYSODIC_PASSKEY_RP_ID`) to the public HTTPS origin.
